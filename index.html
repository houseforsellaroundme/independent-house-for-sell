About This Property

This is an independent residential house available for sale in Karri Satyavathi Nagar, Tadepalligudem, Andhra Pradesh. The property is a 2 BHK configuration with a built-up area of 1,100 sq ft on a plot measuring 2.88 cents.

The house is east facing with a north-east entrance, has 2 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, and includes parking for one vehicle. Water supply is through a borewell.

The property is approximately 8 years old, and municipal property tax is stated as paid. The house is being offered directly by the owner, so there is no broker involvement.

The asking price is ₹32 lakh. The property is located in Karri Satyavathi Nagar, Tadepalligudem, near the Government Hospital, with two temples in the vicinity. Tadepalligudem is a municipality in the West Godavari district of Andhra Pradesh, known for its residential neighbourhoods and local amenities.

Proposed Tadepalligudem Airport

The Andhra Pradesh government has initiated a pre-feasibility study for a proposed airport in Tadepalligudem. This is part of the state's broader infrastructure development plan for the coastal Andhra region.

According to a report published by The Hindu, the pre-feasibility study was launched to assess the viability of constructing an airport in the Tadepalligudem area. This indicates that the government is actively considering infrastructure development in this region.

For people who buy property in Tadepalligudem now, this development could mean:
